Carousel Studio Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I post a multi-page carousel on LinkedIn?

Click "Export PDF". On LinkedIn, click the "Document" icon when creating a new post and upload the generated PDF. LinkedIn automatically renders the document as an interactive swipeable carousel slider.

How do I post on Instagram?

Click "Download PNG Zip". Unpack the zip file to receive individually numbered 1080x1350 vertical PNG images (the native 4:5 Instagram aspect ratio) ready for multi-photo gallery posts.

Where is my API key stored?

Your key never touches our servers. It is stored exclusively in your browser's private localStorage under byok_api_key and transmitted directly from your client machine to Google's or OpenAI's endpoints via TLS.

Which AI models are supported?

The studio supports Google Gemini (defaulting to gemini-2.5-flash for high-speed sub-second generation) and OpenAI API endpoints (including gpt-4o-mini or custom OpenRouter models).

AI Provider & API Key Settings
